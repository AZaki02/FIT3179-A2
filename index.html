Some history

Australia has always been historically known for its thriving live music scene. Throughout the 70s and 80s, bands like AC/DC, Midnight Oil, and INXS saw international acclaim and solidifed Australia's position as the home of heavy rock. In recent years, the music scene of Australia is considerably more diverse, with hundreds of different music festivals around the country, catering to various different genres. This page aims to vizualise how diverse the Australian music scene is, by focusing on its range of venues, artists, genres, and demographics.
